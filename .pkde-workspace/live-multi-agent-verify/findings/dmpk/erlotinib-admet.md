## Implications
- **Prioritize In Vitro Kinetic Solubility & Salt/Formulation Screening**: Because ESOL predicts low free-base aqueous solubility (LogS = -4.176 {source: raw/admet/erlotinib.predict.json $.endpoints.solubility.predicted_logS}), experimental kinetic and thermodynamic solubility assays across physiological pH (including salt forms such as the hydrochloride salt) should be prioritized alongside dissolution profiling.
- **Prioritize Microsomal Stability and Time-Dependent CYP Inhibition Assays**: Moderate predicted metabolic stability warrants human and preclinical species liver microsomal/hepatocyte intrinsic clearance ($CL_{int}$) measurements. Furthermore, because rule-based CYP screening does not capture mechanism-based or time-dependent inhibition (TDI)—a known consideration for terminal alkynes (`C#C`)—experimental CYP3A4/CYP2D6/CYP2C9 TDI and reactive metabolite trapping assays are recommended.
- **Confirmatory Permeability and Patch-Clamp Testing**: Descriptor-based high passive permeability (Egan egg) does not account for active efflux (e.g., P-gp/BCRP) and cannot substitute for bidirectional Caco-2 or MDCK assays. Similarly, absence of an Aronov hERG structural flag has a documented false-negative rate and does not replace an in vitro hERG patch-clamp IC50 assay.

## Caveats & Confidence
- **Predictions Are Not Measurements**: All five endpoints in [raw/admet/erlotinib.predict.json](../../raw/admet/erlotinib.predict.json) are deterministic 2D descriptor and SMARTS rule-based classifications, not trained machine-learning models or experimental measurements.
- **Endpoint-Specific Model Limitations**:
  - *Metabolic Stability*: Gleeson (2008) rules classify liability risk from LogP and aromatic ring count; they do not predict in vivo clearance, half-life, or specific metabolic soft spots (such as $O$-demethylation of the 2-methoxyethoxy side chains or alkyne oxidation).
  - *CYP Inhibition*: Simple MW/LogP/charge heuristics miss mechanism-based inactivation and compounds near the MW > 400 Da boundary (erlotinib MW is 393.44 {source: raw/admet/erlotinib.predict.json $.molecular_descriptors.mw} Da).
  - *hERG Liability*: The Aronov (2005) pharmacophore rule has a documented false-negative rate; erlotinib satisfies the basic nitrogen and aromatic ring criteria and sits within 0.29 LogP units of the 3.7 {source: raw/admet/erlotinib.analysis.json $.thresholds_applied.herg_logp_min} threshold.
  - *Solubility*: ESOL predicts intrinsic free-form solubility only and does not model ionization state or salt forms.
