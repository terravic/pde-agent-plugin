## Implications

- **Target Validation and Modality Positioning**: Although `EGFR` is not classified as strictly haploinsufficient by LOEUF (0.5047 {source: raw/genomics/EGFR.gnomad-constraint.analysis.json $.metrics.loeuf} vs. configured `loeuf_constrained` threshold of 0.45 {source: raw/genomics/EGFR.gnomad-constraint.analysis.json $.thresholds_applied.loeuf_constrained}), the point estimate (`oe_lof` = 0.4056 {source: raw/genomics/EGFR.gnomad-constraint.analysis.json $.metrics.oe_lof}) and intermediate pLI (0.3891 {source: raw/genomics/EGFR.gnomad-constraint.analysis.json $.metrics.pLI}) indicate substantial purging of heterozygous LoF alleles in the human population (`obs_lof` = 58 {source: raw/genomics/EGFR.gnomad-constraint.analysis.json $.metrics.obs_lof} vs. `exp_lof` = 143.0114 {source: raw/genomics/EGFR.gnomad-constraint.analysis.json $.metrics.exp_lof}). Program decisions must not equate this `indeterminate` verdict with unconstrained LoF tolerance.
- **Cross-Disciplinary Context for Safety and Chemistry**: Population LoF constraint reflects selection against constitutional heterozygous loss from conception rather than adult pharmacological inhibition. However, because `EGFR` sits near the LOEUF boundary (`oe_lof_percentile` = 18 {source: raw/genomics/EGFR.gnomad-constraint.analysis.json $.metrics.oe_lof_percentile}) and has known physiological roles in epithelial homeostasis, therapeutic strategies targeting `EGFR` in oncology benefit from mutant-selective or tumor-targeted designs to maximize therapeutic index over wild-type `EGFR` inhibition in normal tissues.
